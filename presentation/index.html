01 / 07
Problem

Same fire, two sensors, fragmented truth

MODIS and VIIRS see the same landscape with different resolution, confidence, and overpass times. There is no shared answer to “how intense was this week?”

  • Double-counting or missed co-detections
  • Multi-year global archives freeze the UI or erase sparse regions
  • Hotspot counts and energy (FRP) rarely appear on one panel

Who is it for?

Fire managers, disaster coordinators, researchers, and local teams working with open FIRMS data.

Solution + rule

One map · one calendar · clear thresholds

Load a FIRMS archive or live NRT; match MODIS↔VIIRS in space–time; read activity, FRP, quality, and alerts in one console.

Matched

Both conditions → Matched (High Confidence). Greedy: each MODIS takes the nearest unused VIIRS.

Quality

Share of matched points with confidence > 80. VIIRS: h→100, n→50, l→20.

Exact charts

DuckDB daily/monthly totals are exact; the map stays smooth via spatial sampling.
