Problem · Slide 2 / 10

Same fire, two sensors, fragmented truth

MODIS and VIIRS see the same landscape with different resolution, confidence, and overpass times.

  • Double-counting or missed co-detections
  • No shared answer to “how intense was this week?”
  • Counts and energy (FRP) rarely share one screen
  • Managers jump between portals instead of one calendar

Who is it for?

Fire managers, disaster coordinators, researchers, and local teams using open NASA FIRMS data.

02 / 10
Solution · Slide 3 / 10

One calendar for activity and power

Harmonize

Co-locate MODIS & VIIRS within 1 km and ±6 hours into Matched / MODIS-only / VIIRS-only classes.

Visualize

Point map + dual-axis chart: hotspot counts and Fire Radiative Power (FRP) together.

Compare

Pick any 2023–2026 window and compare to another period in one click.

Regions first (Turkey, Aegean, Mediterranean, California…) with custom coordinates when needed.

Why extracts?

Worldwide dumps are multi-GB. Compact regional files keep the live demo fast while staying faithful to FIRMS fields.

Method · Slide 5 / 10

From detections to a single story

1

Ingest MODIS + VIIRS for the bbox & dates

2

Match within 1 km · ±6 h

3

Aggregate daily counts + FRP

4

Paint FRP-scaled points on the map

Map sampling

For dense seasons we keep an FRP-priority monthly sample so the map stays readable and loads quickly — without erasing regional signal.

Product · Slide 6 / 10

What the live app shows

Controls

Year chips, custom dates, region presets, custom W/S/E/N coordinates, period compare.

Chart panel

Toggle Matched / MODIS / VIIRS / FRP, 3–7 day smoothing, linear or log FRP scale, optional fill.

Map

Small FRP-scaled dots, chunked paint for speed, click a point for FRP and sensor.
