Problem · 2 / 7

Two sensors, one fire — fragmented answers

MODIS and VIIRS disagree on resolution, confidence, and overpass timing.

  • Double-counting or missed co-detections
  • Hard to compare seasons (“worse than last year?”)
  • Counts without intensity (FRP) miss the real story

Users

Fire managers, responders, researchers, and local teams on open NASA FIRMS data.
